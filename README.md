# Awesome AI Rap Video

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of AI tools that generate rap and music performance videos — from photos, lyrics, or finished songs.

> Maintained by the [RapWizard](https://rapwizard.site) team. Every entry is checked against the vendor's public site (last checked **2026-10-27**); no invented benchmarks, no affiliate links. PRs welcome.

## Photo → rap performance

You have a photo (yours, a friend's, your dog's) and want the clip where it gets up on stage and performs.

- [RapWizard](https://rapwizard.site) — 1–2 photos in, full performance out: original written verses, generated rap vocals, 808-led beat, six-plus stage scenes, duet mode with preset partners (alien / robot / pet characters). 5–15s vertical, watermark-free at every tier, credits from $2.99.
- [Spitnova](https://spitnova.com) — same category, second opinion: photos into preset rap scenes, solo or duo, 5/10/15s, credit-based.
- [Zoice](https://zoice.com) — photo lip-synced to a track you already recorded; your words, your voice.

## Lyric / idea → short clip

- [RapVideo](https://rapvideo.io) — a lyric line plus a scene idea rendered into a 4–15s clip with generated audio; free first render (watermarked).

## Finished song → music video

- [Freebeat](https://freebeat.ai) — song link or file in, beat-synced video out, fast; built for volume.
- [Neural Frames](https://neuralframes.com) — audio-reactive generative visuals that pulse with your track.
- [Kaiber](https://kaiber.ai) — stylized, artistic music videos with timeline editing.

## Generate → edit → publish

- [Revid](https://revid.ai) — generation, editing and social-publishing workflows in one place.
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com) — cinematic generated shots you direct and edit yourself; deepest control, most work.

## How to choose

Pick by the input you already have:

| You have | Use |
|---|---|
| One or two photos, no track | Photo-first tools (RapWizard, Spitnova) |
| A photo + your own recording | Lip-sync tools (Zoice) |
| A lyric and a scene idea | Text-to-clip tools (RapVideo) |
| A finished, mixed song | Song-driven tools (Freebeat, Neural Frames, Kaiber) |
| A plan to edit and post variations | Editor workflows (Revid, Runway) |

The four checks that matter on every tool, including ours: **vertical output, vocals in or out, watermark policy, cost per render.**
